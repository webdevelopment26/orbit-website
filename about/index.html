Our story

People first. Standards high. Delivery honest.

We help professionals grow, and we strengthen the institutions that stand behind them: colleges, unions, and employers who believe in craft and fairness.

Our mission

Orbit Training and Development Ltd exists so people can reach their potential without losing their principles. We build skill, poise, and judgement for teams who operate under scrutiny.

Alongside one-to-one and cohort work, we run field-based initiatives that connect professional education with the wider workforce, including closer ties between colleges and trade unions in the countries and sectors where we are active.

How we work

  • Listen first. We map your context, constraints, and proof of success before we propose a format.
  • Design with purpose. Every session serves an outcome. No generic pack dropped onto your organisation.
  • Deliver with credibility. Facilitation is candid, respectful of experience, and aimed at what happens Monday morning.
  • Follow through. When it helps, we embed consolidation habits so new practice sticks instead of fading after one event.

Who we support

Individuals, employers, colleges, and unions that care about professional standards and healthy workplaces. If that sounds like your circle, we would like to hear from you.
